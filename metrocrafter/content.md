title: Metro*Crafter*
badge: Being built, one station at a time

İstanbul's Metrobüs, the M2 metro and Marmaray, rebuilt in Minecraft and run by the people who ride them.
Not a plugin added to a server, but a whole server of its own, where a city's daily commute becomes the game.

## A city that runs on its players

::: station
name: Take the driver's seat
label: Drive

Drive a metrobüs down its own lane or a train beneath the Bosphorus, stop after stop, with the doors and
the bells you already know.
:::

::: station
name: Hear every stop
label: Listen

Every station has its name, and the voice in the carriage says it as you pull in.
:::

::: station
name: Keep the city moving
label: Work
change: yes

Drivers, guards, cleaners, the chiefs who keep a line on time: everyone has a job, and the city notices
when it's done well.
:::

::: station
name: Have a say
label: Vote

The city chooses who runs it, and what they decide changes the ride for everyone.
:::

::: extension
name: Not yet boarding

MetroCrafter is early, and it is being laid down carefully. There are no sign-ups and no dates yet; when the
first passengers can board, this is where it will say so.
:::
